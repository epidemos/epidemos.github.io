What We Provide

Services

From survey design to automated reporting, we provide end-to-end epidemiological support for complex humanitarian and public-health contexts.

Survey Design & Mobile Data Collection

With many years of expertise in conducting complex surveys in challenging contexts, we design surveys according to rigorous epidemiological methods, deliver training for enumerators, and set up mobile data collection to reduce error and time-lag.

Monitoring & Surveillance Systems

Whether you need basic project M&E or a full early-warning alert and response (EWARS) system, we identify core indicators, ensure enumerators are trained, set up data collection, and enable real-time dashboards and on-demand reporting.

Automated Analysis, Visualisation & Reporting

Using the latest open-source technologies we build customised interactive analysis, visualisation and automated reporting workflows. From one-off surveys to long-running monitoring programmes, you always have the latest intelligence at your fingertips.

Training & Capacity Building

We offer courses in humanitarian epidemiology, outbreak investigation, scientific communication, and reproducible research using R, Shiny, and R Markdown — building lasting local capacity wherever we work.
