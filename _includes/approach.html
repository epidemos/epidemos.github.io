Our Approach

Three principles guide everything we do.

Rigorous Science

Every engagement is grounded in the most current epidemiological methods — from study design and sampling to statistical analysis and interpretation.

Open-Source Tools

We work entirely with open-source technologies — R, Shiny, and R Markdown — ensuring full transparency, reproducibility, and accessibility across every project.

Capacity Building

We believe the most impactful work transfers knowledge and skills to local teams — building the expertise that outlasts any single project.
